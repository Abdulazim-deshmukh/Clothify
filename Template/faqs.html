Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about shopping with Clothify.

How do I place an order?

Create an account, browse the shop, add products to your cart and complete the checkout form.

Do I need to pay online?

No. This project intentionally keeps checkout simple and does not include an online payment gateway.

Can I update my cart quantity?

Yes. Open your cart, change the quantity and select Update.

How do I request a return?

Please review the return policy page for the basic return process used by this demo store.
